Featured research

Explainable Transformers for time-series forecasting

One model forecasts hourly series at many locations, explains each prediction, and works where there is little or no training data. The application is river flow, driven by weather and landscape data.

1Inputs

Large-scale time-series forecasting

Ph.D. research · Clemson University · 2022–2026
  • One Temporal Fusion Transformer trained jointly on 15 U.S. East and Gulf Coast regions, not one model per river.
  • 12 time-varying weather inputs (precipitation, temperature, humidity, wind, pressure, radiation) over a 30-day lookback.
  • 27 static catchment attributes: topography, land cover, soil, and climate.

2Model: Temporal Fusion Transformer

The model selects the inputs that matter, encodes the past, attends across time, and outputs quantiles. Hover over or tap the numbered points to explore each component.

  1. Static covariate encoders. Catchment attributes condition every part of the network, so one model adapts to each river.
  2. Variable selection. Learns which weather inputs matter at each time step. This is where the feature importance comes from.
  3. LSTM encoder–decoder. Encodes the 30-day past and decodes known future inputs to capture local temporal patterns.
  4. Gated residual networks. Let the model skip nonlinear processing it doesn't need, which keeps it efficient.
  5. Interpretable multi-head attention. Finds long-range dependencies, and its weights show which past hours drive each forecast.
  6. Quantile outputs. The 10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles give every 1–24 h forecast an uncertainty band.

3Output

Probabilistic, explainable forecasts

  • 30% lower RMSE than NOAA's National Water Model (v3.0), the operational U.S. forecast system.
  • Zero-shot transfer: the trained model forecasts at 10 gauges it never saw, which helps in data-scarce catchments.
  • Calibrated uncertainty from quantile regression, which matters for flood warnings.
  • Explainable: attention and variable selection show which drivers matter in each region.
